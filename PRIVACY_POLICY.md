# Privacy Policy for मी सचिन मलिक - Mi Sachin Malik (com.misachinmalik)

**Effective Date:** October 14, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 14, 2026  

This Privacy Policy describes how the **"मी सचिन मलिक - Mi Sachin Malik"** mobile application (Package Name: `com.misachinmalik`), managed by the official office of Sachin Malik ("we", "us", or "our"), collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application.

By using this application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly:
* **Account & Profile Information:** When you register or login, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, ward/booth, and assembly constituency.
* **Grievances & Civic Issue Reports:** When you report an issue, we collect the title, category, description, location/address details, and any media (photos or attachments) you submit to describe the issue.
* **Communication & Feedback:** If you reach out to us via contact forms, WhatsApp, phone, or email, we collect the details you provide in your message.

### B. Automatically Collected Information:
* **Device & Push Notification Data:** We collect unique device identifiers and push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging / FCM) to send you real-time updates regarding grievance redressal, notifications, upcoming events, and news.
* **Log Data:** Basic diagnostic information in case of app crashes or performance issues.

---

## 2. Device Permissions Requested

To deliver key civic features, the app requests the following system permissions on your mobile device:
* **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`):** Required to capture real-time photos of civic problems (e.g. road repair, garbage, street light issues) when submitting an issue.
* **Photos / Media Storage (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`):** Required to select and upload photos from your device gallery when reporting an issue.
* **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Required to send notifications regarding the status of your reported issues, civic updates, news, and official announcements.

*Note: You can grant or revoke these permissions anytime through your device settings.*

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for public service and civic engagement purposes:
1. **Grievance Resolution:** To verify, track, investigate, and resolve the civic issues and complaints submitted by citizens.
2. **Citizen Communication:** To notify you about status changes on your complaints, answer queries, and provide updates.
3. **Public Updates:** To share news, press releases, social media updates, and announcements about local public works, developmental initiatives, and community events.
4. **App Security & Functionality:** To ensure smooth functionality, prevent fraud, and maintain app stability.

---

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy. **We do not sell, rent, trade, or commercially monetize your personal data.** 

Your data may only be shared under the following conditions:
* **Administrative & Field Workers:** Authorized local representatives, administrative coordinators, and field teams assigned to resolve the reported civic issues.
* **Third-Party Service Providers:** Trusted cloud infrastructure and messaging services (e.g., Firebase / Google Cloud, Expo) that assist us in sending push notifications and hosting backend data securely. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality and data protection obligations.
* **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, court order, or governmental authorities to comply with applicable regulations.

---

## 5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All communication with our servers is secured using industry-standard TLS/SSL encryption.

---

## 6. User Rights & Data Deletion Policy

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:
* **In-App Account Deletion:** You can delete your account and personal records directly inside the mobile app at any time by navigating to **Settings (सेटिंग्स) > स्टोरेज व खाते > खाते हटवा (Delete Account)** and confirming the deletion request.
* **Email Deletion Request:** You may also submit an account deletion request by contacting us at **contact@sachinmalik.com** with your registered mobile number and subject *"Account Deletion Request"*.
* **Data Removal Timeline:** All personal identifiable data, profile details, and submitted issue logs associated with your account will be permanently purged in compliance with Google Play Store data safety guidelines.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The **"मी सचिन मलिक - Mi Sachin Malik"** application is intended for citizens and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove the data.
